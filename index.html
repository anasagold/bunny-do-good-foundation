About Bunny Do Good Foundation

Bunny Do Good Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation based in the Vaal and serving children and communities across the country. We believe every child deserves to know that they are loved, valued and wanted.

What We Do

Family & Children

We help orphaned children reconnect with their families and work alongside people and organisations committed to protecting children.

Opportunities

We expose children to educational opportunities, historical places, travel and experiences that broaden their view of what is possible.

Community Support

We work with local businesses, volunteers, churches, social workers, police and other organisations to strengthen support for children.

Hope & Faith

We encourage children to grow in faith and become wholesome young people through the Word of God.
